## Part 1 — the prototype (what we learned, what we keep)

### Locked design decisions
- **PIVOT 2026-10-14: farming is cut.** The castle forecourt is the café; the game is designing it. Ingredients come from fights, machines are free-placed on the grid in a build mode, servants auto-work one machine each and the player fixes jams.
- **PIVOT 2026-10-16: conquest, not delves.** One small café on the summit of Mount Meowtterhorn; critter factions hold the regions below (raccoons in Pinewhisker Woods, dogs in Purrgatory village, mice/rats in Squeakmoor fields, frogs/toads in Croaking Fens, seagulls/crabs in Gullport harbour). Conquer region by region via battle nodes + a boss gate on a Cuphead/Overcooked-style mountain map (cat token walks paths; conquered tiles flip to castle crimson). Each conquered region: a branch shop lot + its cats become customers to turn into vampire servants. More servants = café runs itself = more time to fight (battles cost in-game time). Win = three meters: Land 5/5, Vampires (all cats turned), Chain 6/6 shops. The delve combat became the battle arena.
- ART STYLE: clean flat vector (Cult of the Lamb look), not pixel art. UI font Fredoka. UI palette: ink #2a1a2e, cream #fff3dc, paper #e8dcc7, crimson #c0243a, moss #7fcf6a, gold #f2c14e. UI is deliberately loud: letter-in toasts, crimson borders, tilted cards.
- 2026-10-16: the caped design (sleek fur, yellow eyes, no fangs, crimson cape, crescent pendant) is the SERVANT look; customers keep the fluffy fanged design.
- MUSIC SYNC: 112 BPM; prop idle = still first frame + slow side-to-side rock on the beat (3°, 2 bars, neighbours in opposite phase); the active row plays only when used. Trees pingpong frames forwards then backwards, never mirrored (M17).
- CONTROLS: E use (hold = repeat; churn/scoop/fix are ring holds), Q build mode, Tab (build: next machine; book: pages), Shift sprint/roll, H household panel, K book (skill tree / progress / recipes), T show-and-tell, X undo build, 1-4 spells, Enter map/home. Key-chip bar fades in after 1 s still; prompt chip over the locked target. Gamepad: A use, X build, RB tab, B sprint/roll, LB staff, Start teach, Y descend/map.
- TARGETING: reach 2.4; rank 0 machine usable now, 1 customer, 2 machine with nothing to do; score = rank×10 + dist − 0.5×facing-dot.
- Camera: one camera, one controller, one grid for café, map and battle. Café zoom 1, battle 2.05 (leans 0.6 toward the cat), map 2.2.

### Café systems (as shipped in the prototype)
- Machines (data/machines.json): barrel 30c (fish, cycle 6, cap 6), churn 60c (fish→base), counter 80c (scoop + hand over), sprinkle shaker 40c, cold box 35c (cap 4, stores cones), bin 10c, dehydrator 30c (waffles only, cap 6); tier 2 needs conquered land: smokehouse 120c (fish→smoked), velvet churn 160c (smoked→velvet base), waffle iron 110c. Decorations tipjar 15c, menuboard 25c. fixSec 2, wearPerCycle 0.08; a machine at 100 % wear jams until fixed (hold E).
- Orders are spelled `<flavour>[+velvet][+waffle][+sprinkles]`; extras pay velvet 14, waffle 10, sprinkles 6; an order fits a cone with every asked extra; unasked sprinkles tip 3; a bare cone for a topping order is rejected. Flavours plain / catnip / nightberry (picker card at the counter, pre-picks the first queued order). VIP customers (land ≥ 1): chance 0.35, patience ×1.6, price ×1.5, velvet/waffle orders, crown overlay.
- Customers queue below the counter with order + patience bar; serving while green = Perfect → favour; 5/5 favour → the cat joins as a servant. A cat whose patience runs out leaves and costs village favour. Shop hours 10–18; a day is 9 real minutes (dawn→dusk 4.5).
- Servants are walking agents (game/staff.ts): plan of machine ids, carry, level 1–10 (4×level xp per level, 1 xp per sale/drop-off). Direct order from the household panel (H): station + next station. Show and tell (T): the servant trails you, records every machine you use, T again teaches the plan (cut to level+1 non-store machines). From level 5 a servant fixes jams on its plan (4 s). Idle servants lounge in front of the castle door. Branch staff cost 30 coin/day each, max 3 per branch; branches pay at dusk.
- Café alerts (bottom right, derived from state each frame, max 5): jammed machine, customer under 30 % patience, servant holding something with no store, empty barrel by day.
- Market (06–18) at the forecourt stall: base prices with ±25 % daily seeded swing; buy fish (→ emptiest barrel), catnip, nightberries, sprinkles.
- Recipe book: ??? until a customer orders it, steps with missing machines in red with cost/lock, a star once served. 11 recipes (shop + VIP).
- Save: autosave at every new day and on close; VERSION 4 {world, shop, conquest, skills}.

### Conquest & map
- data/regions.json: 6 bands (summit z 0..8, then woods, village, fields, fens, harbour to z 68); nodes cafe/battle/boss/lot with x/z; tiles held/won; enemies/boss/specials; market goods; lotCost 150. Map walk limit = unlocked bands with wobbled borders (±2.5). Node use labels: "Raid …", "Clear N more outposts to reach …", "Storm …!".
- Secrets per region: chest coin once, patch item daily (fish → barrel), lost cat joins the household (or 30 coin). Revealed within 5 tiles, collected within 1.
- Patrols: 2 + floor(i/2) per open unconquered region per day, amble 1.2 t/s, chase 2.8 within 4.5 tiles, touch → one-wave fight; routed until the next day; 3 s grace after a fight.
- Map life: instanced scenery per region (camera-facing), cloud shadows, flock fly-pasts, fog bank over closed regions that puffs apart on unlock, signposts where a path enters a region, fireworks from the café after a fresh win, landmarks animate on conquest (flag swap, boss heap collapses, lot kiosk rises).
- Knock-out in a fight: clock advances 2 h, loot lost, wake at the café.

### Battle (Cult-of-the-Lamb-style dungeon runs)
- A node = BattlePlan {kinds, layers: RoomKind[][], boss, tier, night, specials, obstacles, shape}. Rooms fight / elite / treasure / rest / boss, exit after the last layer. Doors per next-layer option open when the room is cleared (treasure/rest at once); 1 door = north, 2 = west+east, 3 = west/north/east, never the entry side. Rooms are shaped (unions of circles and rects: box, glade, bean, hollow, long, cross, clover, nook, arena), themed per region (floor/edge/out tiles, border/low/fill props, decor, obstacles), laid out beside each other with a 4.5-tile corridor; the last 3 rooms stay visible.
- Player: 5 hearts (+Nine Lives), claw 3-hit combo (window 0.8; heavy third: +1 dmg, knockback ×1.7, lunge 9, cooldown 0.55; each swing lunges 5), hit-stop 0.06/0.11 s, screen shake, damage numbers; roll chain of three (window 0.35), third = pounce (0.4 s at 16 t/s, +0.15 s invuln) then 0.75 cooldown; rolls are invulnerable; hurt invuln 0.8 s. Blood 3 (+perks), refills 1/6 s and +1 per KO; spells on 1-4 bypass shields.
- Only telegraphed attacks hurt: chase critters wind up 0.45 (red stripe) → lunge → recover 0.5; a hit staggers a windup. Ranged keep 3.5–6.5 away, windup 0.6. Shields block from the front, turn 2.2 rad/s only while moving, lunge once facing you then exposed 1.3 s; backstab (dot < −0.3) +1 dmg + stagger 0.7. Swoop (bats/gulls) orbit r4, ring, dive through, dizzy; dives sooner if rushed (M20). Burrow (diggers/moles) = unclawable mound, rumble + red zone, burst within 1.2, dazed 1.6. Wisp keeps 4–6.5 away, homing flame, blinks every 2nd hit. Bones: first KO leaves a pile 3 s that one hit smashes, else back up at maxHp−1. Gloom: goo slows to 55 %, slam ring 1.9, splits into 2 gloomlets.
- Bosses (M20): moves cycled from `enemies.<boss>.moves` — charge (dazed 1.6 s on a wall), volley (5 bags, spread 0.3), slam (windup 1.0 → hurts within 2.6 → recover 1.5 = the claw window), hop (leaps to where you stood, lands with a slam + goo), burrow (tunnels 1.8 s unclawable, rumbles 0.6, bursts = slam). Rummage charge/volley 32 hp; Barkley charge/slam 24; Rat Baron burrow/volley 32; Old Toadstool hop/volley 32; Gullbert charge/volley/slam, flies, 32. All whistle for help at 66 % / 33 % (3 of the region's first critter kind); enraged under 33 % (pause ×0.6, windup ×0.8, faster charge).
- Dens: woods (Bin Barricade, Acorn Lookout, Raccoon Den 7 layers), village (Kennel Gate, Butcher's Yard, Barkley's Doghouse 7), fields (Grain Store, Scarecrow Line, the Baron's Burrow 8), fens (Lily Landing, Reed Beds, Old Toadstool's Pond 8), harbour (Fish Market, the Jetty, Gullbert's Lighthouse 9). Specials: woods all five; village bonerat/gloom; fields digger/bonerat; fens gloom/wickling; harbour bellbat/wickling (chance 0.3, elite 0.6, from layer 1, one per room).
- Blessings (treasure rooms, cards 2.4×3.4 that hover/spin/sparkle, E take / Esc cancel): sharp (+1 claw), fleet, heart (+1 max), lucky (+coin drop). Campfire heals 2. Night fights: +1 hp per critter, +1 pickup per room. Loot: per-kind drop tables, 1–3 floor pickups per room; xp = max hp of each knocked-out critter (kept win or lose).
- Hand-made room layouts (data/rooms.json via the in-game F2 editor): used 60 % of the time for fight/elite rooms, always for boss rooms; spawn markers any/special/boss.
- Skill tree (data/skills.json): heart + 12 nodes in 4 arms. N Moon: Thin Blood +2 blood, Moonbeam (2 blood, cd 2.5, line 7×1, 3 dmg), Blood Moon +1 blood/KO. E Fang: Sharp Fangs +1 claw, Blood Frenzy (3/12, 6 s claw cd ×0.5, every 4th hit heals), Rend +2 heavy. S Shadow: Nine Lives +1 heart, Shadow Step (1/3, blink 4.5, invuln 0.4, 2 dmg r1.6), Mist Form roll cd ×0.6. W Court: Silver Tongue +20 % coin, Bat Swarm (2/6, 3 homing bats 2 dmg), Night Shift servants +25 %. xpBase 25 × level per level, 1 start point.
- Balance from 40 seeded bot runs per den (bot attacks only stunned bosses): woods 9/40 won, village 24, fields 38, fens 5, harbour 1 — humans do far better; tune from play.

### Engineering rules that paid off (keep in UE)
- Every tunable number lives in data, never in code. A balance change is a data diff.
- Every system simulates headless; a game day simulates in under a second; seeded runs are deterministic. 150+ seeded bot dungeon runs per change caught every soft-lock.
- One camera, one controller, one grid. World geometry generated; everything with a silhouette is a sprite/billboard.
- Perf: never re-upload shared textures per frame; initialise textures at load; adaptive render scale under 55 fps.
